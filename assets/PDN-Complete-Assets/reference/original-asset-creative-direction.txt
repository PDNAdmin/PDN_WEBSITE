## 2. LOCKED CREATIVE DIRECTION

**Visual world: Onyx & Gold Sculpture**

PDN exists in a cinematic, dark, silent studio where precious objects float or rest on black stone.

**Materials**

- Onyx Black: `#0F0F10`
- Pishon Gold: `#D4AF37`
- Matte-to-satin black stone with subtle grey veining
- Polished and brushed warm champagne-gold metal
- Warm gold-tinted volumetric lighting and subtle atmospheric haze

**Signature element: The River**

The River is a smooth, continuous gold tube of constant thickness that follows the official PDN brand motif.

When the River appears, it should enter and exit through the composition's boundaries rather than terminate randomly inside the frame.

Use the official logo guide to maintain recognizable brand geometry.

**Photography and rendering**

- Premium photorealistic 3D
- High-end product photography aesthetic
- 50–100mm lens characteristics
- Dramatic lighting with soft falloff
- Realistic surface textures and reflections
- Fine cinematic grain
- Generous negative space where required

**Strict exclusions**

No text, random letters, watermarks, people, hands, faces, UI screenshots, unrelated symbols, neon colours, chrome, plastic, cartoon rendering, or generic futuristic imagery.

Every asset must look like it belongs to the same premium PDN visual world.
